# Idea scoring rubric

Score each candidate idea 1–5 on seven criteria. Weighted total = Σ(score × weight),
so the total also runs 1–5. Weights sum to 100%.

| # | Criterion | Weight | 1 = low | 5 = high |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Novelty / white space** | 20% | A current (<2 yr) review or several papers already cover this well | No recent synthesis; a genuine gap or fresh angle |
| 2 | **Feasibility with current resources** | 20% | Needs data, IRB, funding, or collaborators you don't have | Doable now with material on hand |
| 3 | **Clinical / public-health impact** | 15% | Niche curiosity | Changes counseling, policy, or practice for many |
| 4 | **Visibility / expected citations** | 10% | Unlikely to be read or cited much | Likely to be cited and discussed |
| 5 | **Strategic fit** | 10% | Off your line of work | Builds your photoprotection thought-leadership / grant narrative |
| 6 | **Competitive risk (reverse-scored)** | 10% | Others are clearly about to publish this | Little sign anyone else is close |
| 7 | **Effort-to-payoff** | 15% | Large effort for a modest paper | Small effort for a solid, useful paper |

> Criterion 6 is scored so that **5 = low risk**. Check preprint servers
> (medRxiv/bioRxiv) and recent conference abstracts before scoring it.

## Weighted total → action

| Total | Action |
|---|---|
| ≥ 4.0 | **Green-light now.** Assign an article type and target journal; start. |
| 3.0 – 3.9 | **Develop.** Firm up the angle, pre-pitch if it's a review, or gather the missing data. |
| 2.0 – 2.9 | **Park.** Record it; revisit at the monthly re-score. |
| < 2.0 | **Kill.** Keep the row with `status = killed` and a one-line reason. |

## Worked example

> Idea: narrative review of visible-light photoprotection endpoints in melasma.
> Scores — novelty 3, feasibility 5, impact 4, visibility 4, strategic fit 5,
> competitive risk 3, effort-to-payoff 4.
> Weighted = 3(.20) + 5(.20) + 4(.15) + 4(.10) + 5(.10) + 3(.10) + 4(.15)
>          = .60 + 1.00 + .60 + .40 + .50 + .30 + .60 = **4.0 → green-light**,
> contingent on an editor pre-pitch since it's an unsolicited review.
