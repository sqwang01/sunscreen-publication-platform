### Idea 1 — The SPF label crisis has crossed the Pacific: what US dermatologists should say before it lands here
- **Topic:** spf-testing-metrology
- **Why now:** Australia's independent-testing scandal escalated this window — CHOICE re-testing put 16/20 sunscreens below their labelled SPF, Ultra Violette voluntarily recalled all batches of Lean Screen SPF50+ (independent labs returned SPF 4–64 across eight tests), and Naked Sundays, Aspect, Endota and others paused sales in consultation with the TGA. NPR ran a lay explainer on why "SPF is complicated" on 2026-09-06. No US dermatology-journal commentary yet frames this as a test-method-reproducibility problem (ISO 24444 in vivo variability) rather than a single bad actor — and it lands right as bemotrizinol enters US multi-filter formulations that will need the same in vivo testing.
- **Proposed article type:** Viewpoint / Commentary (no new data; controversy + test-metrology angle)
- **Evidence needed:** literature only — ISO 24444 inter-lab variability literature, the CHOICE/TGA public record, the FDA in vitro/in vivo final-order test provisions
- **Target journals:** JAMA Dermatology (Viewpoint) → Australasian Journal of Dermatology (Viewpoint/Commentary — home-region relevance, likely faster) → Photodermatology, Photoimmunology & Photomedicine (Invited Commentary/Editorial)
- **Budget (from journals.csv):**
  - *JAMA Derm Viewpoint:* body ≤1200 w (≤1000 if 1 small table/figure) | no abstract | refs ≤7 | figs+tables ≤1 | checklist none. AI/LLM may not draft opinion pieces.
  - *Australasian J Dermatol Commentary:* specs **TBD** — confirm at https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/14400960/homepage/forauthors.html
  - *Photodermatol Photoimmunol Photomed Invited Commentary/Editorial:* 2000 w incl abstract + legends, excl refs.
- **Rubric (quick):** novelty 4 / feasibility 5 / impact 4 / visibility 5 / leadership 4 / risk 3 / effort 5 → **4.40**
- **First action:** Pre-pitch a JAMA Derm editor with a 3-sentence scope note (angle = test-method reproducibility, not brand-shaming); in parallel pull 8–10 ISO 24444 inter-laboratory variability papers.

### Idea 2 — A 50-state map of pediatric UV-protection law: what the Gandhi et al. review implies for advocacy
- **Topic:** pediatric-pregnancy (also skin-cancer-prevention)
- **Why now:** Gandhi et al. (*JAAD*, 2026-09-03, PMID 42692111) is a fresh cross-sectional review of US state legislation on pediatric UV protection built from LegiScan — school sunscreen-possession laws, shade mandates, tanning-bed bans for minors.
